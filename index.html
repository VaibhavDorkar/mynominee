Understand the problem

What could go unclaimed?

Financial assets may remain unclaimed when people forget about them, records are lost, or families do not know they exist.

Bank deposits

Savings accounts, fixed deposits and other bank balances.

Insurance policies

Policies or benefits that beneficiaries may not know about.

Investments

Shares, dividends, mutual fund investments and related holdings.

Missing records

Financial documents or account details that are difficult for family members to locate.
